│ CODE APPROVAL WORKFLOW                                                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

WHEN CELSIUS AI WANTS TO CHANGE CODE:

1. You'll see a new request in "Code Approvals" tab
2. Click on the request to view details
3. Read the analysis:
   - What files will change
   - What benefits you get
   - What risks exist
   - What alternatives were considered
4. Make your decision:
   - APPROVE: Green button → AI implements change
   - REJECT: Red button → AI drops the change
5. Add notes to explain your reasoning

REMEMBER: Celsius AI CANNOT change code without your approval!
